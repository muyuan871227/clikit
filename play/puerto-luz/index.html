Puerto Luz

Two stories. Your next move.

A mobile puzzle romance for women, designed for Latin America. Meet Mateo and Gabriel, use their distinct puzzle skills, and win each level to continue their stories.

Play the prototype
No download or account needed. Best played in portrait on a phone. Tap two neighboring pieces to match three; change the game language in Settings.
What you can experience

Two male leads, three puzzle-gated chapters each, story choices and illustrated scenes. English, Spanish, Portuguese and Chinese text. Adult characters; non-explicit romance.

Work in progress

This is a prototype, not a released mobile game. The new routes use subtitles without voice acting. Ads and purchases are labeled simulations; no money is charged. Progress is saved in this browser.
